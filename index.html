About Cate

Guiding You Toward Clarity and Resilience

I work with people who are navigating change, difficult seasons, or looking for support as they make sense of what they are carrying. My own experiences with change and difficulty have shaped the way I show up for clients, with warmth, curiosity, humility, and genuine respect for each person's story.

I offer a supportive, collaborative space where you can be yourself and explore what might help you move forward. My approach is primarily person-centered, meaning I believe you are the expert in your own life. I also draw from narrative therapy, EMDR, mindfulness-informed practice, solution-focused therapy, and practical tools from cognitive behavior therapy when they are helpful and align with your goals.

You do not have to have everything figured out before reaching out for support. I aim to offer a supportive, collaborative space where you can be yourself, make sense of what you are carrying, and explore what might help you move forward. I look forward to meeting you and supporting you on your journey.

What I Help With

Support for Life's Most Challenging Moments

Anxiety & Chronic Stress

Living with persistent worry or tension can make even daily tasks feel overwhelming. Together, we'll explore the root of your anxiety and develop practical strategies to help you feel grounded and in control.

Health-Related Challenges

A diagnosis, chronic illness, or health change can impact every part of your life. I offer compassionate support to help you process difficult emotions, adapt to new realities, and reclaim a sense of agency.

Life Transitions

Whether it's a career shift, relationship change, relocation, or loss, transitions can feel disorienting. Counselling can help you navigate uncertainty with greater clarity and confidence.

Emotional Overwhelm

When emotions feel too big to manage, it's hard to think clearly or move forward. I'll help you understand what's beneath the surface and build the emotional resilience you need to regain balance.

Self-Identity & Personal Growth

If you're questioning who you are or who you want to become, you're not alone. I provide a reflective space where you can reconnect with your values, strengths, and sense of purpose.

Burnout

Burnout is more than exhaustion — it's a signal that something needs to change. Together, we'll identify the patterns draining your energy and build a path toward sustainable well-being.

FAQs

Questions You Might Have

Psychological services are available virtually to individuals and couples across Alberta and British Columbia, with limited in-person appointments offered in Grande Prairie, Alberta.

Virtual appointments are conducted through a secure, confidential video platform, providing convenient access to psychological services.

The fee is $200 per 50-minute session. Payment is due at the time of your appointment. If you have questions about fees or billing, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Many extended health benefit plans cover counselling with a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC). I recommend checking with your insurance provider to confirm your specific coverage. I can provide receipts for you to submit directly to your provider.

The first session is about getting to know each other. I'll take time to understand your concerns, what brought you to counselling, and what feels most important to you right now. There's no pressure to share more than you're comfortable with — we move at your pace. By the end, we'll collaboratively outline a direction for our work together.

You can book a free 15-minute consultation or your first session via Psychology Today or by sending an email. I'll get back to you within 24 hours to confirm a time that works for you.

The number of sessions varies from person to person and depends on your goals and circumstances. Some clients find value in short-term focused work (6–10 sessions), while others benefit from ongoing support. We'll regularly check in on your progress and adjust as needed.
